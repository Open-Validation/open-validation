## What the data is

The data shows each English council's local planning validation requirements: the documents a
council needs before it will accept ("validate") a planning application. It is a draft that gets
better as people check it, in three steps:

1. **AI draft:** collected by AI from the council's own published validation list. Nobody has
   checked it yet. Councils still marked "Data scraping in progress" hold an even earlier, rougher
   extract.
2. **Volunteer check:** a person has compared it with the council's published list and found
   that it matched, as far as they could tell, on the day they checked. This is a good-faith check
   by a volunteer. It is not the council's confirmation.
3. **Council confirmed:** someone at the council has confirmed the entry, or the council maintains
   it itself.

The colours on the map and in the tables show which step each council and each cell has reached.
Most of the data is still at step 1.

## Always check the council's own list

Councils change their lists, and the AI or a volunteer can get things wrong. **The council's own
published validation list always takes precedence over this data.** Check it, and the national
requirements, before you submit an application.
